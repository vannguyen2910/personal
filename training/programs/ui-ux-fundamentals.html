The curriculum

What each
session builds.

Foundations
01

Design Thinking Foundations

Learn what Design Thinking actually is, and how UI, UX, and Product Design differ, before you touch a single screen.

Learning objective
  • What is Design Thinking
  • UI vs UX vs Product Design vs Service Design
  • Practice-based design process methods
What you'll achieve
  • A clear map of the 5-stage process applied to your own first project idea
  • The vocabulary to tell UI, UX, Product, and Service Design apart
Empathise
02

Customer Understanding

Learn why you are not your user, and how to run an interview that surfaces real needs.

Learning objective
  • Why you are not your user
  • Making your assumptions visible
  • Basics of a good user interview
What you'll achieve
  • Your assumptions written down and separated from what you actually know
  • Real interview notes from a person outside your own head
Define
03

Synthesis & Problem Framing

Turn scattered notes into a problem statement worth solving.

Learning objective
  • Turning raw notes into patterns (affinity mapping)
  • Writing insight statements
  • How Might We: turning insights into design questions
  • Writing your problem statement
What you'll achieve
  • A clustered set of themes from your own notes
  • One sharp, testable problem statement
Ideate
04

Concept Sketching

Generate real options fast, then pick the strongest one to move forward with.

Learning objective
  • Diverge before you converge
  • Crazy 8s: generating ideas fast
  • Turning rough ideas into concept sketches
  • Picking your strongest concept
What you'll achieve
  • A page full of rough directions, not just your first idea
  • One concept chosen with a reason you can explain
05

Information Architecture Basics

Structure your product around evidence, not guesses.

Learning objective
  • What is Information Architecture
  • Organizing content around evidence, not guesses
  • Card sorting basics
  • Building a simple sitemap
What you'll achieve
  • A sitemap for your own project, grounded in how people actually think
  • A structure you can explain and defend
06

UI Fundamentals

Learn the visual language, hierarchy, typography, color, layout, that makes an interface actually work.

Learning objective
  • Visual hierarchy: guiding the eye
  • Typography and color basics
  • Layout, grid, and spacing
  • Common UI components (buttons, cards, inputs)
What you'll achieve
  • A basic visual language you can apply consistently
  • Enough tool fluency to start building screens
Prototype
07

Prototyping Mindset + Building Your First AI Prototype

Build your first working prototype, using AI as a tool you direct, not one that decides for you.

Learning objective
  • Why prototyping pattern-first avoids inconsistent screens
  • Design tokens and your component inventory
  • The build loop: from tokens to your first working screen
  • Correcting AI output: chat, feedback, and direct edits
What you'll achieve
  • A working first screen built with AI, not just imagined
  • A repeatable build loop you can reuse on the next screen
Deliver
08

Interaction Design Fundamentals & Critique

Design every state your users will hit, and learn to give and receive critique that actually helps.

Learning objective
  • Task flows vs. user flows
  • Designing every state: default, loading, empty, error, success
  • Mapping your critical user journey
  • Giving and receiving critique: "I noticed / I wonder / What if"
What you'll achieve
  • A complete flow for your feature, not just the happy path
  • Practice giving and taking critique without it feeling personal
09

Packaging & Presenting Your Case Study

Turn your work into a case study you can defend in a room.

Learning objective
  • What makes a case study defensible
  • Structuring your story: problem, process, decisions, outcome
  • Presenting a journey, not just screens
  • Whiteboard practice: talking through your process live
What you'll achieve
  • A packaged case study, ready for a portfolio
  • A live walkthrough you've actually rehearsed
Close
10

Reflection & Self-Assessment

See how far you've come since session 1, and set your next goals.

Learning objective
  • Reassessing where you stand, compared to your Session 1 baseline
  • What grew, what's still developing
  • Turning your case study into a portfolio-ready story
  • Setting your next 90-day goals
What you'll achieve
  • A clear before/after picture of your growth
  • A concrete next-90-days plan

Anchored to your own real project throughout. The coaching output is the deliverable, not a separate exercise.

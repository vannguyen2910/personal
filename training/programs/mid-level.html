The curriculum

What each
session builds.

Every session applies to your own real work project. You bring the work-in-progress, and the session is coaching and direction.

Understand
01

Design Thinking for UX

Learn to run the design process yourself, not just follow one.

Learning objective
  • Map the design process end to end, from discovery to delivery
  • Learn when to apply Design Thinking versus other frameworks
  • Identify which phase your current project is actually in
What you'll achieve
  • A clear map of your project against a structured process
  • Your own point of view on what phase comes next, and why
  • Confidence to explain the process to stakeholders, not just follow it
02

Customer Understanding

Ground every decision in real user friction and evidence, not assumption.

Learning objective
  • Learn interview techniques that surface real friction, not opinions
  • Practice separating what users say from what they actually need
  • Build a repeatable way to capture and organise evidence
What you'll achieve
  • Real interview notes from your own project, not hypothetical ones
  • A clear picture of your users' actual pain points
  • Evidence you can point to when a decision gets challenged
Define & design
03

Synthesis & Problem Definition

Turn raw research into a problem statement worth solving.

Learning objective
  • Learn affinity mapping to turn scattered notes into clear themes
  • Practice writing a problem statement that's specific and testable
  • Learn to tell the difference between symptoms and the real problem
What you'll achieve
  • A clustered set of research themes from your own project
  • One sharp, testable problem statement
  • Language you can use to get your team aligned on it
04

Develop Solutions & Ideate

Generate and validate real options before committing to one.

Learning objective
  • Learn structured ideation techniques to generate real options
  • Practice evaluating solutions against the problem, not against taste
  • Learn how to pressure-test an idea before committing to it
What you'll achieve
  • Multiple validated directions, not just your first instinct
  • A clear rationale for the direction you choose
  • Practice defending a design decision with evidence
05

Information Architecture

Structure the product around how users actually think.

Learning objective
  • Learn core IA methods: card sorting, sitemaps, and taxonomy
  • Practice structuring content around user mental models, not org charts
  • Learn to test an IA before it's built
What you'll achieve
  • An IA for your own project grounded in real user input
  • A structure that holds up under usability testing
  • The vocabulary to defend structural decisions to your team
06

Interaction Design & User Flows

Design the full journey, not just the happy-path screen.

Learning objective
  • Learn to map full user flows, not just individual screens
  • Practice designing for edge cases and error states, not just the happy path
  • Learn interaction patterns that scale across a product
What you'll achieve
  • A complete, edge-case-aware flow for your own feature
  • A stronger eye for when a pattern is the right choice
  • A flow you can walk stakeholders through end to end
Validate & deliver
07

AI Prototyping

Move from concept to interactive prototype fast, using AI as your co-pilot.

Learning objective
  • Learn to use AI tools to generate and iterate on prototype concepts
  • Practice going from rough idea to interactive prototype fast
  • Learn where AI speeds you up, and where it still needs your judgment
What you'll achieve
  • A working, testable prototype built in a fraction of the usual time
  • A repeatable AI-assisted prototyping workflow you can reuse
  • Confidence knowing when to trust AI output and when to override it
08

Solution Validation & User Testing

Know your design works before it ships.

Learning objective
  • Learn to design a usability test that answers a real question
  • Practice moderating sessions and capturing unbiased findings
  • Learn to separate real signal from noise in test feedback
What you'll achieve
  • Real usability test results from your own prototype
  • Evidence-backed answers on what to fix before shipping
  • A clear, defensible go or no-go recommendation

Anchored to your own real project throughout. The coaching output is the deliverable, not a separate exercise.

What you walk away with

One end-to-end UX case study, delivered on your own real project, plus the early leadership behaviours that get you noticed.

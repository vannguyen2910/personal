## Why this framing works (for your reference, not for the page)

- **Monthly number, not the full total, is the first thing a visitor reads.** 3.600.000đ reads like a normal coaching rate; 10.800.000đ reads like a decision that needs a committee. The total is still there for anyone checking the math — just not shouting.
- **The group card gives 1:1 permission to cost more.** Seeing 1.800.000đ next to 3.600.000đ makes the 1:1 price feel like a deliberate choice, not an arbitrary one.
- **"Let's talk" over "Enroll" or "Buy now."** Keeps this a consultative first step rather than a checkout — which matters more the higher a price sits versus SkillMall/Neyul's instant-checkout courses.
- **"Starting at" quietly does two jobs:** it's honest that in-person costs more, and it avoids ever having to show a visitor a bigger number before they've talked to you.
